What working
with me looks like

01 · LET’S SAY HI

We chat about your business and what you need. No pressure, no obligations.

02 · A QUICK BRIEF

You answer a few simple questions. No jargon, no homework, just the basics about your business and your clients.

03 · RESEARCH

I study your audience and competitors: what your clients care about, what worries them and what makes them choose one place over another. These insights become the base of your website.

04 · YOUR PROPOSAL

We talk on a call or in chat, whatever is easier for you.

I share what I found and how I’d use it on your website, with a clear timeline and price for your business.

05 · DESIGN, STEP BY STEP

I start with a few options for the first screen, and we choose one together.

Then I design the rest, and we approve it step by step. You can peek into my Figma anytime to see how it’s going. Revisions are unlimited, within reason 🙂

06 · LAUNCH

I build the website in code, publish it and connect your domain if you need one. Then I set up Google Analytics, Search Console and SEO, so people can actually find you.

07 · IT’S ALL YOURS

You get full access and a simple way to edit the site yourself. And I stay in touch: if something goes wrong or you have a question, just write.
